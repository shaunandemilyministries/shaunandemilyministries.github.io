Clipdeck Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Clipdeck is a personal tool used by its owner to manage their own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data Clipdeck accesses and how it is handled.

Data Clipdeck accesses

When the owner connects their YouTube account through Google sign-in, Clipdeck requests access to:

  • View basic channel information, such as the channel name, subscriber count and video count.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel, such as views and audience retention.
  • Upload videos to the owner's own channel. Every upload is approved by the owner first, and is created as a private video with a scheduled publish time.

Clipdeck cannot change the account password, delete existing videos, or access anything outside YouTube.

When the owner connects their Facebook Page through Facebook sign-in, Clipdeck requests access to:

  • Show the list of Facebook Pages the owner manages, so they can choose one.
  • Read basic details of the chosen Page, such as its name and follower count.
  • Create posts on the chosen Page: short video clips as Reels and written quotes as text posts. Every post is approved by the owner first, and is scheduled to publish at a set time.

Clipdeck does not access the owner's personal Facebook profile, friends, messages or other Pages.

Clipdeck does not request or receive Google or Facebook passwords. Sign-in happens on Google's and Facebook's own pages.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own channel performance, to help plan their content, and to upload the videos they approve. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Clipdeck runs on the owner's own computer. Access tokens issued by Google and Facebook are stored encrypted on that computer, with the encryption key held in the operating system's secure keychain. Clipdeck has no central server that collects user data.

Cookies and device storage

Clipdeck does not use cookies or tracking. It stores small display preferences, such as which setup steps are ticked, in the web browser on the owner's computer. These never leave that computer.

Keeping data current, and deleting it

Channel and Page details are refreshed from YouTube and Facebook every few hours. If access is revoked, Clipdeck stops using the data, and disconnecting deletes the stored tokens and channel details from the computer straight away.

Google API Services

Clipdeck's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Clipdeck uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time with Disconnect in Clipdeck, or from the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Facebook access can be removed with Disconnect in Clipdeck, or from Facebook's Business Integrations settings at https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens from the computer.
