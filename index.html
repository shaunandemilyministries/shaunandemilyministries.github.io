Clipdeck

Clipdeck is a personal tool for managing one creator's own social media accounts. It helps plan a week of content from a single long-form YouTube video: suggesting short clips and quotes, scheduling them, and showing performance for the creator's own channel.

It runs on the owner's computer and is not offered as a public service.

Clipdeck uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to show their own channel analytics. By using Clipdeck you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
